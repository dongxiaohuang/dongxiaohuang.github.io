On 7 October 2026 I gave the talk _Data-Driven Game Publishing_ at Kingston University, as part of Tencent’s campus visit.

Publishing a game is a series of bets: which game to sign, where to localise, how to launch, how to keep players. Each bet needs data, and the hard part is access, not volume. I showed how DataBrain, the AI agent platform I lead for Tencent’s global publishing teams, lets people ask those questions in plain English and inspect the evidence.

The case study was PEAK, a $7.99 co-op climbing game that reached about 170,000 concurrent players. Four questions to the agent traced four waves of players, what Steam reviewers valued, the creator videos around each update, and ended in a generated dashboard that refreshes with new data.
