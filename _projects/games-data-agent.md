From 2023 to August 2026 I worked with the global game publishing team at Tencent IEG Global in London. I led DataBrain, the platform the publishing teams use daily to access and analyse games data, built on the overseas games data platform I helped create.

DataBrain’s AI agent turns a plain-English question into evidence in four steps: it works out which game, metric and period the question is about; fetches the right sources, such as market data, Steam, player reviews, creator activity and internal data; connects the signals across them; and answers with charts, sources and generated dashboards that refresh with new data. Publishing teams spend less time gathering data and more time on the decision.

I also mentor AI agent development inside Tencent.
