From March 2021 to July 2023 I was an adjunct lecturer at the School of Software Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT) in Guangzhou, teaching _Natural Language Processing_.

The course is run jointly by Tencent Games Academy and SCUT as part of a New Engineering programme for software talent, and students take it for credit. Eleven Tencent lecturers, from teams including Business Security, the Growth Platform and IEG Global, taught it on campus to give students a practical way into natural language processing. The first run finished in May 2021, and I taught on the course until July 2023.
