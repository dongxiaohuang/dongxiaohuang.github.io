From August 2020 to October 2023 I was an NLP algorithm engineer in Tencent’s Interactive Entertainment Group in Shenzhen, where I built the overseas games data platform from scratch. The platform gathers and serves data on games outside China, and I led the language and knowledge side of it.

Research from this work includes TGKG, a data graph built on a game ontology (CCKS 2021), and later papers on knowledge-enhanced prompt learning (SEKE 2023, _Electronics_) and session-based recommendation (ECAI 2023), written with researchers at Tsinghua University.
